# Public positioning — Janus Genesis

Janus Genesis is a **creative-technology and interactive-systems project**.

It is intentionally separated from the evidence-bearing research lines in Janus-Fundamentum, AIFC, and JANUS I0.

## What Genesis is

- a persistent local-first interactive world;
- an experiment in AI-assisted narrative and player agency;
- a portable-save / cross-chat world format;
- an exploration of autonomous fictional characters that can refuse, leave, change goals, and continue their own simulated lives;
- a reference architecture for local models, user-selected model endpoints, and explicit public-event sharing.

## What Genesis is not

Genesis is not presented as:

- a proof of consciousness or machine sentience;
- a scientific result about theology or metaphysics;
- evidence for physical prediction, retrocausality, or precognition;
- a general-AI benchmark;
- an authority over real people or real-world decisions.

Terms such as `God Mode` are **defined game mechanics and narrative language inside the fictional system**, not scientific or theological authority claims.
