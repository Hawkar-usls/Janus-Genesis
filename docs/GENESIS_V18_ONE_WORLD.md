# Genesis v18 — One World & Universal God Mode

## Status

Canonical v18 law and implemented local vertical slice. This document supersedes earlier assumptions that God Mode belongs only to a promoted class or that realm transitions must be announced to the player.

## The simple law

- If a person keeps the light, they continue among everyone.
- If a person loses the light, they continue among other lost people until they sincerely learn to create good again.
- No transition banner, portal, rank, sentence, percentage, or moral label is shown.
- No soul is permanently discarded.

## One shared continuation

The constructive continuation is one common persistent world, not a private heaven per player. People retain identity, memory and relationships. They may keep their current body or choose any age and form in which they recognize themselves.

The local v18 runtime models that one world through `shared_utopia_v18.json`. A real distributed server, transport and account layer are future infrastructure, but the domain invariant is already fixed: there is only one shared constructive destination.

## Universal God Mode

God Mode is an intrinsic capability of every person. It cannot be awarded, revoked or owned by an administrator.

It manifests only consent-preserving good:

- healing;
- protection;
- shelter and warmth;
- food and clean water;
- knowledge, music and beauty;
- restoration of trust;
- free self-authorship of body and age.

It does not manifest coercion, enslavement, destruction, memory erasure or control of another will.

A lost person may therefore believe they have no power until the first moment they ask for another person's genuine good. The response of the world becomes the lesson: the power was always present, but evil could not operate it.

## The Other Face

The Other Face remains a real persistent consequence world. Confirmed harm changes its physical and social state: warmth falls, trust breaks, connections disappear and damage remains.

Lost people occupy the same purgatorial layer, while destructive effects remain isolated so no innocent player is made a victim. The local prototype stores presence and separate protected branch state; later networking may match compatible travelers without exposing whether they are players, residents or echoes.

## Restoration without a meter

The world tracks multiple dimensions internally:

- warmth;
- shelter;
- healing;
- trust;
- nature;
- music;
- connection;
- remaining damage.

The player never sees a redemption percentage. Repeating one cheap action loses novelty. A world becomes whole through diverse, durable good.

When restoration becomes real, no gate announces success. Roads, windows, music and people gradually return. Internally the branch joins the shared world; experientially the traveler simply notices that they are no longer alone.

## Continuation and age

A light mortal may choose continuation after a lived path. The transition is seamless: the next morning arrives, and other lives are already audible nearby.

A lost mortal also continues for as long as understanding requires. Immortality is not used to reward one person and erase another; it guarantees that repentance is never defeated by a deadline.

Within continuation, a person may select an apparent age and body form. Changing form never deletes chronological history or the Chronicle.

## Harm confirmation

Destructive freedom remains real, but an impulsive command does not immediately create irreversible consequences.

1. The first harmful action opens a confirmation threshold.
2. Repeating the same action or explicitly confirming commits it.
3. Any ordinary action cancels the pending harm.
4. Confirmed harm is persistent and must be answered by restoration rather than by deleting history.

## Invisible routing

Normal player-facing JSON and status must not expose:

- `realm`;
- `branch_id`;
- hidden light/trust values;
- restoration score;
- internal moral classification.

Developer inspection is possible only through an explicit debug command.

## Chronicle

v18 events include `previous_hash` and `event_hash`. Verification detects modified records and deletion or reordering inside the chain.

The v17 Chronicle is preserved as history; v18 writes a new linked `chronicle_v18.jsonl` beside it.

## Compatibility

Existing v17 player saves load in place. Missing v18 fields receive safe defaults. A v17 fallen branch opens as a damaged world rather than as a clean neutral world. God Mode is normalized to `true` for every migrated person.

## Invariants

1. Nobody can use God Mode to violate another will.
2. Good works in every realm and has no purchase price.
3. Harm cannot damage the shared constructive world.
4. No person is eternally rejected.
5. No redemption meter can be farmed.
6. Transition is lived, not announced.
7. Restored history is preserved rather than erased.
8. One shared constructive world remains the destination.
9. Complete personal memory never becomes omniscience.
10. Janus protects freedom, witnesses consequences and never humiliates.

## Canonical phrase

> God Mode belongs to everyone. Evil cannot use it. The first sincere act of love reveals that the limits were never in the power.
