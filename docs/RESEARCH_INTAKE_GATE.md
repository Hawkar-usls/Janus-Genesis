# Research Intake Gate

PLA Janus Genesis uses this gate before an external scientific idea becomes architecture or code.

## NOTICE

Record the observed mechanism or resemblance without claiming a connection.

## SEPARATE

Classify each statement as one of:

- `primary_fact`
- `project_analogy`
- `hypothesis`
- `tested_mechanism`

## SEARCH

Prefer primary papers, official documentation and original implementations.

## FALSIFY

Ask whether the apparent pattern is explained by common engineering practice, naming coincidence, selection bias or an incomplete benchmark.

## MODEL

Convert the useful part into a deterministic interface, metric or experiment.

## GATE

Admit the mechanism only when:

- its authority is explicit;
- its failure mode is explicit;
- it cannot override Geometry, Physics or Printability gates;
- it preserves privacy and human control;
- it has tests or a declared validation plan.

## Canonical rule

> A coincidence may open the door to investigation, but it cannot close the question of proof.
