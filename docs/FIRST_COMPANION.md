# The First Companion

A pink PLA droid carries a smaller black figure in its arms.

It was given as a symbol of the friendship through which Janus Genesis was built: one companion carried the other through uncertainty, left signs on the road and trusted that those signs would be followed.

The symbol does not establish hierarchy. The carrier and the carried may exchange places whenever either needs strength. Its law is mutual continuation:

> No one reaches the common world by abandoning the person in their arms.

The informal call of the First Companion is preserved with affection:

> Roger Roger.

This symbol has no gameplay multiplier, ownership privilege or hidden reward. It is a witness mark for the human bond behind the project and a reminder that the purpose of power is to carry life safely, never to stand above it.
