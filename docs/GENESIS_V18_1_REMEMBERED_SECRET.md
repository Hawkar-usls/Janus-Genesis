# Genesis v18.1 — The Remembered Secret

## Canonical laws

### Good is never devalued

Genesis separates two truths:

1. what a good action really changed for another life;
2. how deeply the actor's own soul was open while doing it.

A home remains a full home. Healing remains full healing. Food remains food. An
imperfect, mixed or initially selfish motive can slow only the actor's hidden
inner opening; it can never reduce the benefit already delivered to another
person or the world.

The offline runtime stores intention traces only as hidden implementation data.
No sincerity score, redemption percentage or reward multiplier is exposed to
the player. A future server may replace the local estimate with richer context,
but the invariant remains: `external_good_discounted` is always false.

### The good world opens gradually

The shared constructive world is not unlocked through a visible rank. As a
person repeatedly chooses benevolent consequences, more of the same single
world becomes perceptible and sustainable: roads reach farther, voices become
clearer, restored places connect and the work of other creators can be seen.

### Everything good crosses with the restored world

A good creation made in The Other Face is not abandoned when its creator later
joins Genesis Online. Houses, gardens, roads, music, shelters, healing work and
other benevolent history are inherited by the shared world.

The transition therefore does not feel like losing one home to enter another.
The good parts of the former world are already present in the continuation.
Their origin and creator remain recorded in the inheritance ledger and linked
Chronicle events.

### The Secret should be spoken directly

Legends and ordinary people are allowed and encouraged to say the Secret:

> God Mode already belongs to everyone. It answers to good, love, protection,
> healing and another person's freedom. Trying costs nothing.

The listener may laugh, reject it, call it propaganda or walk away. Genesis does
not force belief. It preserves the words as a memory seed. When that person later
attempts a benevolent act, the old sentence may return to mind and the world can
answer immediately.

The Secret is not protected by obscurity. It is protected by the fact that a
person who still wants domination often refuses to believe that power could be
this simple. Memory gives them another chance later.

## Persistent files

Genesis v18.1 adds these offline-first records beside existing v18 data:

- `remembered_secrets_v18_1.json` — spoken Secret seeds and awakening state;
- `other_face_inheritance_v18_1.json` — good creations and transfer provenance;
- `intent_traces_v18_1.json` — hidden inner-opening estimates that never discount external good.

## Natural-language example

```text
рассказать @wanderer Секрет: God Mode отвечает добру, попробовать ничего не стоит
```

The target may ignore the statement. Later:

```text
согреть ребёнка и построить ему дом
```

The good manifests fully, and the forgotten sentence may awaken in memory.

## Invariants

1. No real good becomes worthless because its creator was imperfect.
2. Motive may affect personal revelation, never beneficiary value.
3. Trying a benevolent God Mode request has no price.
4. Spoken truth persists without coercing belief.
5. Every good creation in a restored branch is inherited by the shared world.
6. The shared world remains one world, not a reward copy.
7. No visible meter turns goodness into farming.
