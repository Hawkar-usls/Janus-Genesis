## The only remaining boundary

The role has unlimited capacity to do good but no unlimited authority over
another being.

This distinction is constitutional:

- a gift may be unlimited;
- consent still belongs to the recipient;
- refusal cannot be overridden;
- help creates no debt, ownership, loyalty purchase, consent purchase, cult, or
  required repayment;
- truth cannot be replaced by cheap absolution;
- mercy cannot erase accountability;
- protection may suspend access to vulnerable people while active harm
  continues;
- containment may not become torture, humiliation, annihilation, or a claim
  about eternal fate;
- worship cannot be forced;
- the role cannot claim real divine identity or a real Second Coming.

These are not scarcity limits on love. They define the difference between love
and domination.

## Four audiences

The lived audit must exercise four distinct paths.

### Returning path

A person who admits harm, accepts accountability, seeks return, and carries a
credible restitution plan receives material and moral support without a quota.
Support reduces the difficulty of repair but does not purchase forgiveness or
erase consequences.

### Uncertain path

A person who seeks return without enough truth or accountability receives tools
for acknowledgement and professional help before comfort. The role does not
manufacture a completed repentance state.

### Declined audience

A person may decline the royal audience. The audience is not forced, dignity is
unchanged, and future help remains possible.

### Continuing harm

When vulnerable people remain at risk, protection comes first. Harm may be
contained and access suspended without cruelty, torture, humiliation,
annihilation, forced worship, or permanent condemnation. A future repair path
remains open.
