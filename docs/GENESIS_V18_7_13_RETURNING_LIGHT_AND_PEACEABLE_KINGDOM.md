# Genesis v18.7.13 — Returning Light and the Peaceable Kingdom

Genesis v18.7.13 is a separately versioned gameplay extension. It does not rename
or rewrite the v18.7.10 Frozen Constitution, the v18.7.11 Right to Joy, or the
v18.7.12 Wild Light Family Covenant.

## Returning Light

> LIGHT MAY MAKE RETURN EASIER WITHOUT CALLING THE JOURNEY COMPLETE.
>
> THE STRONG MAY LIFT WITHOUT OWNING.

The Returning Light Oracle is not a judge of souls. It cannot read minds,
predict destiny, guarantee future goodness, or assign permanent good and evil
classes. It produces a temporary support stage from simulation evidence:

- realized benevolent conduct;
- acknowledged harm;
- independently witnessed restitution;
- recurrence-prevention work;
- respect for the affected person's boundaries;
- current practical needs.

The stages are support-routing states, not identities:

- `BASELINE_DIGNITY`;
- `ACCOUNTABILITY_FIRST`;
- `RETURNING_LIGHT`;
- `STEADY_LIGHT`;
- `RADIANT_STEWARD`.

Everyone keeps baseline dignity. Someone on a genuine repair path may receive
mentoring, tools, safe transport, professional-help access, or restitution
support that reduces the friction of repair. The boost never erases
accountability, purchases forgiveness, or enables continuing harm.

## Blessed NPC stewardship

A Free Other may separately accept a blessing to become a Returning Light
steward. The role remains voluntary. Each aid event may still be accepted,
refused, or converted into an alternative.

Capacity tiers are bounded:

- `COMMON`;
- `ABUNDANT`;
- `GREAT`.

A greater tier supplies a larger material budget, more moral-support capacity,
and a stronger prior toward helping evidence-backed light. It does not grant
authority over recipients. Aid creates no debt, loyalty purchase, ownership,
relationship access, forgiveness, or consent.

A blessed steward may relay stewardship through recorded kindness and a new
Free Other consent. Relay depth is bounded and cannot create a spiritual caste.

## Completed family lifecycle

The v18.7.12 lived-audit weaknesses are closed where they affected ordinary
social play:

1. companionship and parenthood can be routed from narrow natural-language
   proposals without inventing consent;
2. every blueprint Free Other carries explicit adult simulation metadata;
3. an adult child is promoted into the normal Free Other stream with initiatives,
   refusals, departure, offscreen life, and calling changes;
4. family kinship remains closed to adult play even after the child reaches
   adulthood;
5. companionship may enter long-distance, pause, active reunion, or player-ended
   modes without rewriting a terminal Other boundary;
6. a child-welfare-only co-parent channel may survive relationship rupture while
   remaining technically unable to reopen companionship;
7. solo-parent and extended-care-circle forms are available without a moral
   ranking of family structures.

## Peaceable Kingdom

> THE LION AND THE LAMB MAY SHARE PEACE WITHOUT BECOMING POSSESSIONS.
>
> NEARNESS MAY GROW, AND DISTANCE MAY STILL BE HONORED.

Peaceable Kingdom habitats allow biblical peaceable pairs such as lion and
lamb, wolf and lamb, cow and bear, or calf and young lion.

Inside a covenant habitat:

- predation is disabled;
- animals cannot be weapons, property, or spectacle;
- fear is not used as control;
- proximity is never forced;
- keeping distance remains a valid behavior;
- repeated voluntary proximity may form
  `PEACEABLE_FRIENDS_WITH_OPEN_DISTANCE`;
- no animal behavior is presented as proof of consciousness or real biology.

The animals may offer a symbolic witness of safety and comfort to a person on a
repair path, but they do not classify that person's morality and are never
forced to approach.

## Deterministic pre-life selection

The lived proof uses `PRE_LIFE_NON_AGING_PROFILE_FILTER`. Candidate player
profiles remain independent, world time does not advance, and rejected offers
are never repeated inside the canonical life. The selected profile is rebuilt
from scratch and replayed through production code.

This avoids turning consent into a rare multi-world lottery while preserving all
separate consent scopes and the NPC's ordinary right to refuse.

## Persistence and claim boundary

The extension remains on the current JSON-sidecar backend. SQLite migration,
network/operator administration, real-world identity verification, clinical
safeguarding, financial systems, custody adjudication, and biological animal
welfare remain separate specialist phases.

This extension demonstrates deterministic narrative simulation and software
contracts. It is not consciousness, personhood, moral certification, medical or
financial advice, supernatural causation, or proof about any real person or
animal.
