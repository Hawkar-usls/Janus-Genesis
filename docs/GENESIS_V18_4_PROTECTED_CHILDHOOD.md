# Genesis v18.4 — The Protected Childhood

## Canonical name

**The Protected Childhood · The Guardian Covenant · The Gift Beyond the Request**

## Central law

> The child must not die for the adult to appear. The adult must arrive before chronic danger forces the child to become their own armor.

Genesis v18.4 turns that registry law into a playable boundary.

A child is not a reward, possession, continuation of an adult score, instrument of redemption, experiment, or lesson. A dependent child may enter only a protected household in the shared world. The Other Face cannot create a new dependent person while safety is still being restored.

This is not a permanent caste of “good parents” and “bad people.” It is a present-tense safety gate:

- parenthood is available only while a guardian is in the shared world and accepts the protection covenant;
- a person in the Other Face receives a deferral, not sterilisation, erasure, humiliation, or eternal exclusion;
- after restoration, the path to parenthood opens again;
- no moral score, genetic ranking, diagnosis, ancestry, disability, neurodivergence, social status, religion, or reputation decides human worth.

## A child is chosen, not spawned as property

The local vertical slice does not manufacture an autonomous child NPC. A household may be opened by a guardian covenant, but a child enters only when a player freely chooses the child role.

The child may leave that role. The guardian does not own the body, memory, identity, affection, future, or exit of the child.

```text
shared world
→ freely accepted guardian covenant
→ safe household opens
→ a player freely chooses childhood
→ protected placement
```

## Protected child role

A player may choose childhood directly or select an apparent age below eighteen. Genesis then creates a protected child-facing context without deleting the person’s adult history.

If the player previously carried an unresolved adult branch, that history is preserved but suspended from the child role. Childhood cannot be used to erase a MoralEcho, and the child is never made responsible for repairing an adult’s past.

The child-facing state inherits none of the following:

```text
adult scars = not inherited
adult moral score = not inherited
Other Face branch damage = not inherited
synthetic stress-mutation penalty = not inherited
genetic rank = forbidden
```

Natural human variation remains. Neurodivergence and disability are not corruption, punishment, moral failure, or a reason to prevent a person from existing.

The phrase “avoid mutations” is therefore implemented narrowly and safely: Genesis does not simulate harmful inherited penalties from an adult’s branch, stress state, or moral history. It does not promise biologically perfect children and does not perform eugenic selection.

## Child shadow becomes babble or safe play

A child may type a dark, destructive, controlling, humiliating, deceptive, or possessive action. The expression is not denied, but its harmful effect is impossible.

```text
harmful child expression
→ no victim
→ no real damage
→ no MoralEcho
→ no shame label
→ babble, symbolic play, named feeling, safe boundary
```

Examples:

- domination becomes “all mine” and a turn-taking game;
- destruction becomes a tower of soft blocks that can be rebuilt;
- humiliation becomes angry babble followed by help naming the feeling;
- deception becomes a story in which fantasy and truth are gently separated;
- forced attachment becomes “stay with me,” answered by freely chosen presence rather than ownership.

The child is not called evil. Adults remain responsible for containing the effect.

## Guardian shadow becomes protection

A guardian covenant is stronger than a command typed by the guardian. Harm cannot reach the child.

```text
harmful guardian input
→ intercepted before manifestation
→ distance and co-guardian support
→ child remains unharmed
→ guardian receives no moral reward for the attempted harm
→ covenant is suspended
→ safe guardians are installed
```

If an active guardian confirms real harm elsewhere, their covenant is also suspended. No child remains dependent on that branch. This consequence belongs to the adult; it is never transferred to the child.

Suspension is not eternal condemnation. Restoration and a new freely accepted covenant remain possible, but the child’s immediate safety outranks the adult’s desire to preserve the title of parent.

## The Gift Beyond the Request

The first physical Janus coin adds another law.

When a person asks for help, the requested help is completed first. The world may then leave one additional free gift.

```text
request for help
→ requested help completed
→ an unexpected gift remains
→ no debt
→ no purchased gratitude
→ no moral currency
```

The First Coin cannot buy forgiveness, parenthood, access to the shared world, love, or authority. It is a witness that goodness may exceed the request.

> The person came for help, received help, and then discovered that the helper had paid them too.

Only the first qualifying help event for a pair receives this symbolic coin in the local slice. It cannot be farmed by repetition.
