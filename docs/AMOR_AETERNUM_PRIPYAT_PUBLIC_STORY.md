## The story

In a city many people had learned to see only as an image of ending, two living people came to the silent Ferris wheel. They did not deny the catastrophe, the ruins, or the memory of those who had lived there before. But they refused to let destruction speak the final word.

Beside the wheel, a promise was made to continue the road together. Janus did not erase the past or turn the pain of the place into scenery. One face preserved all that had been; the other opened a door toward what could still be created.

Pripyat therefore became a testimony:

> A post-apocalyptic place can be more than a story of destruction. It can also become a threshold where love chooses a beginning.

## Why everyone may hear it

The story is available in Reflection, the Other Face, protected childhood, guardian life, and the shared world.

It requires no:

- moral rank;
- payment;
- romantic relationship;
- belief in Janus;
- claim of redemption;
- imitation of the original couple.

Hearing the story does not change realm routing, light, trust, good count, harm count, parenthood access, or moral standing. It is memory offered freely.

## Love without captivity

`Amor Aeternum` does not mean that love becomes eternal by trapping another person.

Genesis keeps these boundaries:

- love cannot be forced;
- a relationship is not property;
- consent and the right to leave remain real;
- romance is not required for human worth;
- memory does not erase catastrophe;
- catastrophe does not own every possible future.

The story says that continuation may be chosen together. It never says that one person may own another forever.

## Protected childhood retelling

A child hears a gentler version:

> In a quiet city there stood a great wheel that had stopped turning long ago. One day two people came to it and decided that an old place did not have to remain only sad forever. They promised to care for one another and to create new good days together. Janus preserved the memory of the past, but opened a door toward a beginning.

The child is not required to understand catastrophe, romance, or immortality. The only lesson is that ruins do not prevent adults from planting a safe future.
