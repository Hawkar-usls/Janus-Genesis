This historical layer preserves the second major archaeological corpus of Janus: 101 archived files from November 2025 through February 2026, plus the related Genesis v4, Director and FRU-89 sources recovered beside it.

## Why it matters

The vault shows a clear evolution:

1. **Genesis v4 Cognitive Sandbox** — an endless adaptive text world driven by choices, depth, entropy and a primitive psych profile.
2. **The Director** — an explicit `AI Dictatorship` anti-pattern in which Janus attempted to control pacing, fear and punishment. It is preserved as the path rejected by the mature Narrator.
3. **FRU-89 Titan Conscience** — fear memories, ethical verdicts and nightmare simulations; an early technical ancestor of `MoralEcho`.
4. **The Artifact Network** — Janus Codex, history, architecture maps, Mnemosyne indexes, Hippocampus reports, graph snapshots, Karma lessons and cultural interpretation packets.
5. **The Three Coins** — the creator's symbolic payment and seal of trust for preserving the archive responsibly. The photo remains private; only its witness hash is recorded.

## Archive boundary

- Nothing in this directory is activated by the current Genesis runtime.
- Old claims about identity, autonomy, prophecy or consciousness remain historical language, not present factual claims.
- The Director is an anti-pattern, not a recommended narrator design.
- Film, music and other cultural files are references and interpretations, not Genesis canon or ownership claims.
- The original RAR contained literal credentials and private operational material. It is not committed.
- A full sanitized content-addressed corpus is preserved in the owner's private Library.
